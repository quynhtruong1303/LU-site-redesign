The heart and soul behind Fran's Flowers

Meet Francine Miller

Francine "Fran" Miller opened the doors to Fran's Flowers in 1995 with a simple dream: to bring more beauty and joy to her beloved Mission District neighborhood. What started as a small corner shop has blossomed into a community cornerstone.

Growing up in San Francisco, Fran spent her childhood helping her grandmother tend to her backyard garden. Those early mornings among the roses, dahlias, and sunflowers sparked a lifelong passion for flowers and their power to brighten even the darkest days.

After studying horticulture at UC Davis, Fran returned home with expertise and vision. She wanted to create more than just a flower shop—she wanted to build a place where neighbors could celebrate life's moments, big and small, with fresh, beautiful arrangements.

Community First

From day one, Fran has believed in giving back to the community that embraced her dream. That's why 10% of every sale goes directly to organizations fighting homelessness across the Bay Area. "Flowers bring joy," Fran says, "but nothing brings more joy than helping our neighbors in need."

Thirty Years Strong

Three decades later, Fran still arrives at the shop before sunrise to hand-select the freshest flowers from local growers. She knows many customers by name and remembers their favorite blooms. "Every arrangement tells a story," she says with a smile. "I'm just honored to be part of yours."
